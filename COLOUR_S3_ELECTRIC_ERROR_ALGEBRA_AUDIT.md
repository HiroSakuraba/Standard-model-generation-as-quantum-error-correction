# The first gauge-compatible error that breaks the four-state passive exchange code

**19 October 2026.** Extends merged PR #59 of the Standard-model-generation-as-quantum-error-correction repository. This is a finite non-Abelian **S3 subgroup of SU(3)** two-vertex model, with four matter qutrits, a six-level oriented link, 486 total basis states and 14 states satisfying both independent Gauss laws. This is **not** the full SU(3) theory or the original nine-quark 21D error-correcting code.

## 1. A stronger negative result: all matter swaps remain passively avoidable

The two on-site colour exchanges A=SWAP(A0,A1), B=SWAP(B0,B1) and a link-dressed cross-site exchange S=SWAP_dressed(A0,B0) each commute with both independent local S3 gauge transformations. In the 14D Gauss-physical space, A²=B²=S²=I. Their generated finite group is **S4 with exactly 24 elements**, so all six pair transpositions (including four across the link) and *every word in those transpositions* belong to it.

A uniform group average over all 24 physical-representation matrices is a projector onto the common fixed (trivial-representation) space. Its rank is **exactly 4**, checked numerically to ≈10^−14 and corroborated by the representation-character count: the global-S3-invariant multiplicity of Sym⁴(sign ⊕ standard) is (15 + 3×3 + 2×0)/6 = **4**.

Consequently, **all matter-pair exchanges in this specific four-qutrit model fail to defeat a 4D passive memory**. This upgrades the weaker three-reflection result in PR #59 and falsifies the idea that simply adding more legal swaps would force active correction. These are symmetry/permutation *code-space* statements, not gate syntheses.

## 2. Introduce a concrete gauge-invariant link electric-energy error

The six-level link is the group algebra C[S3], with independent local gauge transformations acting as the left and right regular group representations. Its central Peter–Weyl projectors are

\[
P_r=\frac{d_r}{|S_3|}\sum_{h\in S_3}\chi_r(h^{-1})\,L_h,
\]

for irreducible representations r=trivial, sign, standard, of dimension 1,1,2. Let **E=P_standard**, which is a **single-link Hermitian projector**. It commutes with *both* local gauge transformations even when tested on arbitrary 486D input, and is therefore a legal **Gauss-preserving electric-link Hamiltonian** for the finite group model. In the physical 14D sector, E has rank **9**, with a five-dimensional zero eigenspace.

Consider the unitary phase error

\[
R=e^{-i\pi E}=I-2E.
\]

Unlike the dressed swaps, this unitary is a **link-only electric representation phase**. An independently specified two-Kraus stochastic fault channel is \(\mathcal N_R(\rho)=(1-p)\rho+pR\rho R^\dagger\), with p=0.37 for comparison to earlier synthetic faults. The π angle and probability are **not measured device noise**.

This is motivated by the electric kinetic term in lattice gauge Hamiltonians, where energies depend on link representation labels. For a finite group the choice of kinetic/Laplacian weighting is nonunique: this work examines *one* symmetry-permitted central projector, rather than asserting that real hardware will specifically generate E with this strength and rate.

Primary sources: Kogut and Susskind, *Hamiltonian formulation of Wilson’s lattice gauge theories*, **Physical Review D 11, 395 (1975)**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.11.395; Mariani, Pradhan and Ercolessi, *Hamiltonians and gauge-invariant Hilbert space for lattice Yang-Mills-like theories with finite gauge group*, **Physical Review D 107, 114513 (2023)**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.107.114513.

## 3. Why passive protection against both classes fails

Compress E onto the exact 4D **common-S4-fixed** sector C:

\[
\operatorname{spec}(C^\dagger E C)=\{0,1/3,2/3,1\}.
\]

The eigenvalues are **not all equal**, and E also has nonzero leakage outside this 4D fixed subspace. Thus E does not act as a scalar on the unique four-dimensional S4-fixed sector. Conversely, E alone has rank-9 and rank-5 eigensectors that *each* support a four-dimensional passive code. The obstruction is therefore genuinely **joint**: it arises from allowing both physically permitted swap interactions *and* electric-link phase errors.

The program also computes the commutant \(\mathcal A'\) of the complex error *-algebra generated by A, B, S and E **on the entire 14D Gauss subspace**. It solves the linear commutator constraints on 14×14 matrices using singular-value decomposition:

| Generator set | Numeric commutant dimension | Smallest nonzero singular value |
|---|---:|---:|
| All S4 matter exchanges (A,B,S) | 24 | Checked in JSON |
| Those exchanges + E | **6** | About 0.511 |

An exact noiseless quantum subsystem of dimension four would require a full \(M_4(\mathbb C)\) logical matrix algebra **inside the commutant**, of complex dimension at least 16. The observed commutant is only six-dimensional, so **no four-dimensional noiseless subsystem exists for this broadened error algebra at the numerical tolerance**. This rules out choosing a different four-state passive subsystem to escape the same joint algebra.

**Proof boundary:** the S4 subgroup size and group-projector rank admit exact representation-theoretic derivations. The 6-dimensional mixed commutant currently rests on double-precision numerical singular-value rank with a nonzero-value gap and does **not** constitute an exact rational/computer-assisted proof. An exact algebraic commutant decomposition is a useful next milestone.

## 4. Does the existing active code now win?

Not as an exact quantum error-correcting code against the entire broadened error set.

The previous 4D \(\omega\)-eigenphase code K was constructed to diagnose dressed S, not arbitrary electric link phases. Apply the **Knill–Laflamme conditions** for the unknown error set \(\{I,S,R\}\): every \(K^\dagger E_i^\dagger E_jK\) must be a scalar multiple of the 4×4 identity. The code explicitly computes all nine compressions and finds non-scalar deviations; the exact conditions **fail**.

For illustrative synthetic π electric phase faults with p=0.37:
- The S4-fixed passive code with **no feedback** has \(F_e=0.630000\).
- The existing 4D active phase code with **no feedback** has \(F_e\approx0.671111\).
- The **ideal, non-microscopic existing dressed-S syndrome and feedback** responds with \(F_e\approx0.681389\).

These values compare **different 4D encodings** under the *same mathematically specified electric fault*, but not the full mixed swap-and-electric noisy channel and not a cost-matched microscopic decoder. The modest ideal-syndrome improvement is not proof of correction of R: its fidelity remains below 1, and the joint KL conditions fail. We do **not** extrapolate this ideal phase-pointer value through the previously studied 495D Hubbard syndrome nor claim any hardware advantage.

## 5. Scope and next experiment

- Every new error operator is **gauge-invariant on both independent vertices**, and the standard-irrep projector acts on a **single link**; gauge compatibility does **not** guarantee protection of logical information.
- The entire matter-swap group is passively avoidable here, so swap-only experiments cannot establish an active advantage.
- A genuinely lattice-gauge motivated electric-link fault breaks that passive protection, but the existing active code is **also not jointly correctable** against this error class.
- No device-specific electric field strength, time distribution, Fock-space microscopic generator, link dissipation, T1/Tphi, 65-syndrome instrument, nine-quark physical implementation or matched architecture cost is established.
- The two-vertex system has one link and **no plaquette**, so the new error is a *link-electric* term and not a magnetic plaquette-flux term. Do not call it a simulated glueball or full gauge field.
- The no-four-dimensional noiseless-subsystem statement applies **only** to this explicitly generated finite-S3 error *-algebra, not to all Gauss-compatible noise, all encodings, or full SU3.

**Next milestone:** obtain an exact algebraic certificate for the six-dimensional commutant, then seek an **active 4D encoding and recovery** satisfying KL for a physically meaningful *restricted subset* of the swap+electric faults, or prove a dimension bound that excludes it. If a code is found, compile a measurement and benchmark microscopic operations, link electric fault rates, and matching passive choices in the same full-occupation/noise model. The goal is a real comparison, not a claim from idealized gate counts.
