4 · What this establishes—and what it does not

Established algebraically. Cubic anomaly identities vanish over the integers. Distance three, three logical degrees of freedom, and the displayed low-weight support counts follow from the stated matrices and finite-field checks.
Only formal at present. The extra generation-sensitive charges are not exact measured Standard Model symmetries. The code coordinates are species labels, not controlled quantum registers with known stabilizer measurements.
Physical obstacle. Generic nonzero quark and neutrino mixing is incompatible with distinguishing generations using fixed, exact diagonal charges. New physics, spurions, or a different operational encoding would be needed.
