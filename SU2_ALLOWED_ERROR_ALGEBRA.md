# SU(2) allowed-error algebra: exact four- and eight-spin audit

**Status: exact finite-model calculation, 18 October 2026.** Companion executable: su2_error_algebra_capacity.py (SymPy >= 1.12). This is **not** a new Standard Model quantum code, a hardware realization, or a decoherence-time estimate.

## The question and its necessary distinction

An SU(2)-invariant operation may act nontrivially inside the singlet multiplicity space. The relevant protection test is not merely whether Gauss's law is respected but whether the specified error set obeys the Knill–Laflamme equations

\[
P E_a^\dagger E_b P=c_{ab}P.
\]

We must distinguish **exact correction of a restricted linear error space** from correction of the **entire unital star-algebra generated by its members** (which allows arbitrary error products). Full-algebra protection is a stronger requirement. All Heisenberg exchanges below are between *co-located* SU(2) fundamentals at the same gauge vertex, not between independently gauge-rotating vertices.

## Four spins: exchange destroys symmetry-only passive protection

The familiar four-spin singlet is two dimensional. In the repository's valence-bond logical basis,

\[
\mathrm{SWAP}_{01}=-Z,\quad
\mathrm{SWAP}_{02}=(Z-\sqrt3X)/2.
\]

Their joint commutant has complex dimension 1, versus 2 for either swap alone. Thus the two allowed, noncommuting pair exchanges generate the full logical matrix algebra \(M_2(\mathbb C)\). Being an SU(2) singlet does not protect its two-fold multiplicity from these physically symmetric perturbations. This independently replicates an existing result in SU2_EXCHANGE_HAMILTONIAN_AND_NO_GO.md.

## Eight spins: one allowed exchange is correctable; two are not

Registers A=(0,1,2,3) and D=(4,5,6,7) are each four-spin singlet qubits. Their tensor product defines a **four-dimensional** code projector P inside the global SU(2) singlet.

For cross-register Heisenberg pair interactions

\[
h_{04}=\mathbf S_0\cdot\mathbf S_4,\quad
h_{15}=\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_5,
\]

exact spin-state contractions give

\[
Ph_{04}P=Ph_{15}P=0,\qquad
Ph_{04}^2P=Ph_{15}^2P=(3/16)P.
\]

Hence **the error space span{I,h04} satisfies exact Knill–Laflamme**, and so does span{I,h15}. This is a mathematical recovery-existence statement; no recovery circuit was implemented.

However, if *either* of the two independently placed errors is allowed, the mixed overlap is

\[
Ph_{04}h_{15}P =
\mathrm{diag}(3/16,-1/16,-1/16,1/48)
=\frac13(-I/4-Z_A/2)\otimes(-I/4-Z_D/2).
\]

This is not a scalar. Therefore **span{I,h04,h15} fails Knill–Laflamme for this four-dimensional encoding**. This does not exclude every smaller subcode, another encoding, or active correction across more physical modules.

## Eight-spin singlet: exact algebra growth

The total-spin-zero sector of eight spin halves has dimension 14. Decomposing two blocks of four into equal block spin \(j_A=j_D=0,1,2\) gives global-singlet sector sizes 4, 9 and 1, using the known four-spin multiplicities 2, 3 and 1.

The six intrablock adjacent transpositions generate the representation of \(S_4\times S_4\). Its operator algebra is

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{intra}}\cong M_4(\mathbb C)\oplus M_9(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathbb C,
\qquad \dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal A_{\mathrm{intra}}=98.
\]

Its commutant is three-dimensional and its block-spin Casimir has eigenvalues 0,2,6 with multiplicities 4,9,1.

Add one cross-register transposition SWAP(3,4). The nearest-neighbor exchange graph becomes connected and generates \(S_8\). The global-singlet representation is the irreducible Young shape (4,4); Schur's lemma and Burnside's theorem give

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{connected}}\cong M_{14}(\mathbb C),
\qquad \dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal A_{\mathrm{connected}}=196.
\]

The exact 14-by-14 rational-matrix commutant has dimension 1. **No quantum subsystem of logical dimension two or more survives arbitrary noise from this *complete* full-matrix algebra.** The assertion is deliberately stronger than the restricted single-fault model above; it does not claim all elementary exchange-error models are uncorrectable.

## Reproduction

Run:

    python -m pip install 'sympy>=1.12'
    python su2_error_algebra_capacity.py

The script uses exact valence-bond wavefunctions (2 and 14 noncrossing pairings), integer/rational Gram matrices, rational commutator-system ranks and symbolic four-dimensional Knill–Laflamme overlaps. There is no numerical tolerance or extrapolation. On 18 October 2026 every assertion passed under SymPy 1.14.

## Relation to the multiplication repository

CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds uses arithmetic **gauges** as computational frame/basis choices, and finite exact certificates to audit complicated constructions. Those gauge choices are not a physical local SU(2) symmetry, and this audit extracts **no QEC code** from integer multiplication. The transferable research practice is explicit allowed operations, small verifiers and counterexample checks; not a claimed physical equivalence.

## Scientific limits and next experiment

- All eight SU(2) fundamentals are assumed at **one** gauge vertex. This is an engineered truncated spin model, not the three-gauge-group Standard Model.
- Algebraic generation of a full noise algebra does not show that long error products occur with significant probability; rates and geometry must be specified.
- The restricted one-error success does not supply an implementation of physical syndrome extraction, recovery or a lifetime advantage.
- Next prioritize locality-restricted graph errors, correlated coupling noise, finite-duration control faults, and noise-adapted recovery in the same eight-spin singlet basis.
