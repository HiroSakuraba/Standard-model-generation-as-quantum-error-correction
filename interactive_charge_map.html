What the checks establish

The structural obstruction

The two singlets have columns (q₀, +Bq₀) and (q₀, −Bq₀) with q₀=(1,1,1,1,1). Subtracting identical fermions across generations gives (0, 2Bq). If that difference lies in the nonzero span of the removed-neutrino difference (0,2Bq₀), a two-coordinate error becomes an undetectable logical error. Choosing B carefully removes every such pair.

All integer cubic anomalies vanish in the chosen full 32-column lattices, because each 16-fermion generation is anomaly free. For p ≥ 5, a transversal cubic gate implements U† on each logical qudit. For p = 3, the exact ninth-root qutrit gate follows from the integral lift.

For p=3, two differential charge rows are insufficient within this architecture: all 1,080 admissible two-dimensional row spaces over F₃ were checked. Three rows suffice. For all p≥5, two rows suffice and one cannot.

What this is not

Fermion species are used as formal qudit labels; this construction does not demonstrate physical quantum information storage inside elementary particles. Family-dependent charges are not exact Standard Model gauge symmetries, and realistic quark and neutrino flavor mixing can break them. These candidate codes have not been shown to outperform two independent 15-qudit codes in distillation.
