# SU(3) plaquette character model: gauge invariance versus quantum-error protection

**Status — 18 October 2026: exact finite-dimensional algebraic experiment plus illustrative numerical spectrum.** This is not a glueball mass calculation, a Yang–Mills mass-gap proof, a full Standard Model encoding, or a physically implemented recovery circuit. Executable certificate: [`su3_single_plaquette_error_algebra.py`](su3_single_plaquette_error_algebra.py). Output: [`su3_single_plaquette_results.txt`](su3_single_plaquette_results.txt).

## Question

Does the gauge-invariant SU(3) Hilbert space necessarily protect quantum information against physically gauge-invariant errors? More precisely, for a specified algebra of gauge-invariant electric and Wilson-loop operators, is there a dimension-two code projector \(P\) satisfying Knill–Laflamme (KL) for all errors in that algebra?

The present answer is conditional and instructive: **a two-dimensional code passes for a deliberately restricted electric-plus-adjoint-loop algebra, while a physically allowed fundamental-loop operator destroys it.** For the full generated electric-plus-fundamental-loop algebra, no nontrivial code can correct arbitrary elements of that complete algebra on this truncated Hilbert space.

## Explicit finite model and source relation

Start from a *single independent SU(3) plaquette holonomy* \(U\). After restricting to gauge-invariant wavefunctions of the holonomy, states are class functions \(\psi(gUg^{-1})=\psi(U)\). Orthogonality of SU(3) characters under Haar measure gives an orthonormal basis \(|p,q\rangle\equiv\chi_{(p,q)}(U)\), where \((p,q)\) are Dynkin labels. This describes a one-loop, character-truncated gauge-invariant sector; it does **not** resolve individual link-edge rishons, Gauss generators, independently fluctuating plaquettes, spatial rotational representations, or dynamical fundamental quarks.

Set the hard cutoff \(p+q\leq2\) and retain, in this exact order,

\[
\{|\mathbf1\rangle,|\mathbf3\rangle,|\bar{\mathbf3}\rangle,
|\mathbf8\rangle,|\mathbf6\rangle,|\bar{\mathbf6}\rangle\}.
\]

The electric Casimir eigenvalues use \(C_2(p,q)=(p^2+q^2+pq+3p+3q)/3\):

\[
D=\mathrm{diag}(0,4/3,4/3,3,10/3,10/3).
\]

The fundamental plaquette Wilson-loop multiplier \(W\) is multiplication by \(\chi_3(U)=\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbf3}U\) **followed by projection to the retained character space**. Its entries are exact integer tensor-product multiplicities, obtained from

\[
(1,0)\otimes(p,q)=(p+1,q)\oplus(p-1,q+1)\oplus(p,q-1),
\]

where terms with negative labels are omitted. Its adjoint is \(W^\dagger=W^T\), multiplication by \(\chi_{\bar3}\) and projection. The Hermitian fundamental magnetic operator is \((W+W^T)/2\), the finite analogue of the Wilson plaquette term in the Kogut–Susskind Hamiltonian. The overall plaquette-link multiplicity and electric/magnetic coefficients are absorbed into a dimensionless scale; they are **not** physically fitted. The six-state truncation, while related by technique, is **not** the link-level minimal qutrit truncation used by Chen, Müller and Yao.

The adjoint Wilson-loop multiplier \(A_8\) is the **correctly truncated** multiplication by \(\chi_8=\chi_3\chi_{\bar3}-1\). It is computed in the *untruncated representation ring* first and projected afterwards. We intentionally do **not** set \(A_8=WW^T-I\), which is generally **wrong near the cutoff boundary** because intermediate representations outside the cutoff can return to retained representations. This distinction has exact regression tests.

## Finite-dimensional algebra results

Using exact rational commutator constraints and repeated matrix-product span tests:

| Allowed complex star-algebra generated by | Dimension | Commutant dimension | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Electric Casimir \(D\) only | — | 10 | Charge-degeneracy sectors; deliberately narrow noise |
| \(D,A_8\) | **8** | **5** | One noiseless logical qubit within a multiplicity-two component |
| \(D,W+W^T\) | **20** | **2** | Charge-conjugation parity remains but offers no nontrivial noiseless subsystem |
| \(D,W,W^T\) | **36** | **1** | The complete \(M_6(\mathbb C)\) matrix algebra, with no nontrivial full-algebra code |

For the adjoint-only algebra, the six dimensions split into a two-dimensional triality-zero sector and a pair of equivalent two-dimensional triality \(+1,-1\) sectors. In algebra notation:

\[
\mathcal A_{D,8}\simeq M_2(\mathbb C)\oplus
\big(M_2(\mathbb C)\otimes I_2\big).
\]

The second term has a two-dimensional multiplicity on which the errors are identical. **Explicit code:** \(|0_L\rangle=|\mathbf3\rangle\), \(|1_L\rangle=|\bar{\mathbf3}\rangle\). For a basis of all eight algebra operators \(B_a\), the verifier checks **every** overlap

\[
P B_a^\dagger B_b P=c_{ab}P.
\]

Thus this is an exact KL *recovery-existence* certificate for the specified complete adjoint-only algebra, not just a check that one operator has a scalar compression. Errors may move the state into \(|\bar6\rangle\) or \(|6\rangle\), so the initial two-state span is not necessarily a passive decoherence-free subspace.

**Negative counterexample:** A fundamental Wilson loop is itself perfectly gauge invariant. Yet on the same proposed logical states its compression is

\[
PWP=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P(W+W^T)P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The second equality gives an especially concrete Hermitian logical \(X\) disturbance. Since the identity and such a loop can belong to the same allowed error linear space, even the simplest KL scalarity test fails. Adding the full fundamental loop multipliers to the electric operator generates \(M_6(\mathbb C)\); its rank-one projectors rule out any code of dimension greater than one correctable against **the whole generated algebra**. The same full-commutant obstruction was checked for the larger \(p+q\leq3\) and \(p+q\leq4\) truncations (10 and 15 states). This is a **finite truncation result**, not a proof about unrestricted continuum SU(3) Yang–Mills theory.

### About triality and physical center symmetry

The equal noise blocks are related by representation conjugation and by the triality \(t=(p+2q)\bmod3\) preserved by **adjoint** tensor products. This restriction is *deliberately imposed on the noise*; it is **not an implication of local SU(3) gauge invariance**. Fundamental plaquette Wilson operators are gauge invariant and change the truncated character triality. In addition, full QCD contains dynamical fundamental quarks, which explicitly break the pure Yang–Mills center one-form symmetry. The artificial center grading of one plaquette's character functions must not be advertised as an automatically protected topological or Standard Model qubit.

## Illustrative low-energy diagonalization and failed energy-level encoding

Consider the dimensionless one-loop toy Hamiltonian

\[
H_L = D-\tfrac12(W+W^T),
\]

with the electric normalization set to one and magnetic normalization set to one. Diagonalizing the *projected* matrices at successively larger representation cutoffs gives:

| Maximum \(p+q\) | Hilbert-space size | Ground energy | First gap |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3 | −0.404071483 | 1.641476300 |
| 2 | 6 | −0.475400015 | 1.376759736 |
| 3 | 10 | −0.480034458 | 1.341671267 |
| 4 | 15 | −0.480144206 | 1.340192294 |

These positive energy gaps are **finite-volume, finite-character-cutoff eigenvalue differences in arbitrary units**. Even apparent cutoff convergence here is not a physical glueball mass, a continuum limit, a thermodynamic limit, or a proof of a Yang–Mills spectral gap.

As a diagnostic, encode in the two lowest eigenstates of the six-state toy Hamiltonian. The electric Casimir alone fails KL scalar compression, with the spectral norm of the traceless part of \(PDP\) equal to approximately **0.816251115**. This concretely demonstrates that simply choosing the vacuum and a lowest excitation as \(|0\rangle,|1\rangle\) does not make a protected qubit. The code reports this diagnostic rather than asserting that the first excitation is a glueball.

## Decision for the wider Yang–Mills and matter-code program

The finite test supports the **negative** conclusion that gauge singlets and a finite excitation gap alone do not provide general quantum error correction. A **positive restricted-noise algebra code** is possible, but its assumption is especially fragile because the standard plaquette magnetic term itself uses the fundamental Wilson loop.

The next substantive step is a **two-plaquette SU(3) link-resolved model** with explicit local Gauss constraints, center-neutral physical observables, and separately measured error/interaction locality. The recent Chen–Müller–Yao SU(3) minimal link truncation is a useful baseline, but must not be treated as equivalent to this six-character one-loop compression. Only a multi-plaquette model with spatial symmetries and appropriate interpolating operators could begin to address glueball spin/parity/charge-conjugation spectroscopy. For a genuinely physical error-correction claim, also specify local error operators, couplings, noise spectra, recovery operations and any permitted nonlocal measurements.
