# Gauge-preserving LOCAL control library for the four-state finite-S3 recovery

**19 October 2026.** Follows merged [PR #64](https://github.com/HiroSakuraba/Standard-model-generation-as-quantum-error-correction/pull/64). That work demonstrated exact *ideal* recovery between time-separated electric-link and matter-exchange faults, using a **42-dimensional three-outcome neutral-pointer unitary** on a 14D simultaneous Gauss-physical system. Its measurement and feedback consisted of global rank-four/rank-four/rank-six projectors, not local executable gates.

**This PR establishes an exact universality/controllability result for a specifically supported, gauge-invariant control alphabet, and an exact finite five-step decomposition for one required cross-link fractional exchange pulse. It does NOT synthesize a finite, optimized sequence for the entire 42D recovery.** No device speed, fidelity, or quantum-memory advantage is inferred from controllability.

## 1. The four individually gauge-compatible control Hamiltonians

The original testbed is a **finite signed-permutation \(S_3\) subgroup of \(SU(3)\)**, with four colour-qutrit matter registers (A0,A1 at vertex A and B0,B1 at vertex B) and one oriented six-state group link. Its 486D Hilbert space has a 14D subspace satisfying the independent Gauss constraints at A and B.

For each of the two six-element matter-exchange error groups used in PRs #63–64, independently tunable continuous pulses are permitted from this small set:

| Control | \(AS\) example | Physical-register support before charged bus |
|---|---|---:|
| On-site exchange | \(A=\mathrm{SWAP}(A0,A1)\) | 2 matter registers |
| Dressed cross-link exchange | \(S=\mathrm{SWAP}_g(A0,B0)\) | 3: link + A0 + B0 |
| Electric-link energy | \(E=P_{\rm standard}(\mathrm{link})\) | 1 link |
| Independently addressable same-colour pair projector on **opposite vertex** | \(Q_B=\sum_{a=0}^2|aa\rangle_{B0B1}\langle aa|\) | 2 matter registers |

For the \(BS\) case the corresponding fourth control is \(Q_A\), and the first control is the on-site exchange B0↔B1.

All four Hamiltonians commute with both independent finite-\(S_3\) vertex gauge transformations. The equality projector is gauge invariant because a simultaneous signed colour permutation preserves whether two local colours are equal; its full 486D off-physical-sector covariance and exact idempotence are checked in code. **Critical limitation:** this basis-dependent equality projector is **not invariant under arbitrary continuous \(SU(3)\)** transformations. The result therefore cannot be promoted to full quantum chromodynamics simply by replacing \(S_3\) notation with \(SU(3)\).

## 2. Exact 196-dimensional local-system controllability certificate

Fix the gauge and use the 14 integer colour-equality-orbit sums from PR #61. On that rational basis, the four Hamiltonians are **14×14 rational matrices**. Their similarity-transformed physical matrices are Hermitian. Define the dynamical Lie algebra generated by

\[
\mathfrak L=\operatorname{Lie}_{\mathbb R}\{iA,iS,iE,iQ_B\}.
\]

Nested commutators of these rational Hamiltonians are either real-matrix or \(i\) times a real matrix, alternating with nesting depth. The test enumerates all independent commutator words by repeatedly bracketing newly added words with each of the original four generators. It uses exact prime-field elimination at **101 and 103**, separately tracking the real and imaginary coefficient types. For **both** error-group choices it obtains:

| Control alphabet | Exact modular Lie rank (each prime) | Conclusion |
|---|---:|---|
| On-site swap + dressed swap + link-electric \(E\) | 21 | Not universal |
| Those three plus same-vertex colour-equality control | 106 | Not universal |
| Those three plus **opposite-vertex** colour-equality control | **196 = 91 + 105** | Full \(\mathfrak u(14)\) |

Because every rational nested commutator is the image of an actual skew-Hermitian Lie word after the physical orbit normalization, **196 linearly independent mod-prime words establish a characteristic-zero Lie rank of at least 196**. No anti-Hermitian 14×14 Lie algebra can exceed real dimension \(14^2=196\). Thus the final rank is **exactly 196**, not a singular-value threshold. The 91 real-skew and 105 imaginary-symmetric dimensions are precisely the standard splitting of \(\mathfrak u(14)\).

As an independent algebra crosscheck, the six-element exchange subgroup's 14D commutant has 52 exact ordered-pair orbit indicators; adding E and opposite-vertex Q produces a rational 392×52 commutator constraint with **rank 51 modulo both primes**. Identity is an exact nullvector, so rank≤51 over the rationals, while modular rank 51 gives rank≥51. Hence **the joint complex commutant is exactly the scalars**, consistent with controllability. A scalar commutant by itself would not prove Lie controllability; the separate 196-dimensional Lie-word certificate is essential.

The conclusion is conditional on a coherent, independently tunable sign/angle control model for those specified Hamiltonians. It does not show that a particular experimental device has the required coupling strength, bandwidth, switchability, negative time access or accuracy.

Primary source for the control criterion: *Quantum optimal control in quantum technologies: Strategic report on current status, visions and goals for research in Europe*, **EPJ Quantum Technology**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-022-00138-x (Lie algebra rank condition).

## 3. A continuous cross-link exchange from only five charged-mediator two-register operations

The sole apparent **three-register** system Hamiltonian S can be replaced with the charged bus already constructed in PR #53:

- A mobile colour-fundamental charged mediator m, with explicit vertex A/B location, is prepared as a stationary fundamental/conjugate-fundamental singlet with a reference at A.
- A gauge-covariant two-register transport \(T\) acts on **link + mobile mediator**, moving its colour through the link.
- A local colour SWAP exchanges the mediator and matter qutrit at its current vertex, using **only mediator + matter**.
- Let \(W=T\,\mathrm{SWAP}(m,A0)\), interpreted in the appropriate matrix-action order. Then the full sequence \(W^\dagger\,\mathrm{SWAP}(m,B0)\,W\) reproduces the *dressed* cross-link matter SWAP \(S\), returning the mediator unchanged.

Because conjugation commutes with the exponential functional calculus, the **fractional exchange pulse** has the *exact*, continuously tunable decomposition

\[
e^{-i\theta(I-S)/2}
=
\mathrm{SWAP}(m,A0)\;T\;
e^{-i\theta[I-\mathrm{SWAP}(m,B0)]/2}\;
T\;\mathrm{SWAP}(m,A0).
\]

This is **five two-register operations** for any selected angle θ: four complete swaps/transports, and one fractional local mediator–matter exchange. The code applies the entire five-step sequence in the original 486D + charged-mediator/reference Hilbert space and checks the final state against the exact dressed-system fractional exchange for angles 0, π/5, π/2 and π. It also checks Gauss acceptance **at every intermediate step** in a nontrivial angle case. No non-Gauss excursion or mediator postselection is used.

This makes the exact **abstract support count** of all four independent system controls at most two registers when the charged mediator is available. It does **not** make T a demonstrated hardware-native operation: transporting a mobile charged particle with the link's parallel transport and preserving its singlet reference has not been synthesized from a device-specific coupling.

## 4. Extend controllability to the three-outcome neutral-pointer recovery

The needed recovery acts on a neutral qutrit pointer and the 14D physical system, total **42 dimensions**. The code provides an explicit gauge-invariant, **two-register** entangling Hamiltonian on pointer plus group link,

\[
H_{\rm int}=|1\rangle_p\langle1|\otimes P_{\rm standard}(\mathrm{link}).
\]

Because E is a projector, its phase gate is implemented exactly as \(I+(e^{-i\theta}-1)|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes E\), without a global 42D matrix exponentiation. The full 3×486D pointer/link action is checked against both independent Gauss transformations on arbitrary input.

If we assume arbitrary **one-register** neutral-pointer \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\) drives, the system's certified \(\mathfrak u(14)\), and tunable \(H_{\rm int}\), standard Lie-ideal commutator arguments yield the entire \(\mathfrak{su}(42)\) and global phase, so the full three-outcome 42D unitary from PR #64 is **reachable in the ideal continuous-control model**. Intuitively: an independently controlled universal 14D system and universal qutrit pointer become jointly universal when coupled by any genuinely nontrivial entangling Hamiltonian of this form. The link-electric projector is non-scalar on the physical 14D space (rank 9), so it supplies that entangler.

**Reachability is not compilation.** This is an existence statement for finite-dimensional closed-system control, not a specified product of finite-length calibrated pulses, an efficient numerical synthesis, or a proof of any upper bound on pulse count. The particular 42D PR #64 recovery (including the flagged six-dimensional complement) still requires an actual gate schedule to be simulated for noise, time, and leakage.

## 5. What's improved, and what remains unresolved

This PR removes a genuine **kinematic / locality obstruction** for the **finite-S3 testbed**: the full recovery target can in principle be built from gauge-invariant controls supported on no more than **two registers at a time** when a mobile charged reference-bearing mediator is allowed. The cross-link continuous exchange previously treated as a primitive is explicitly expressed as five existing charged-bus pair operations.

However the **dynamic and engineering barriers remain**. In particular:

- No solved 42D target compilation or gate counts for the whole instrument. Commutator constructions can be fantastically long or poorly conditioned, so universality must not be read as a practical resource estimate.
- The opposite-vertex pair-equality Hamiltonian is a specific discrete-S3 control, not an SU3-invariant native Standard Model interaction.
- The pointer–link standard-projector coupling is assumed controllable as a two-register Hamiltonian, not physically derived from a device.
- Mobile charge location, singlet preparation, reference lifetime, transport fidelity, clock time, field leakage, logical reset/readout and coherent control errors are not device calibrated.
- The extra mobile mediator and stationary reference add significant hardware and loss overhead not represented in PR #64's nominal four-active-fermion T1 model.
- The actual channel can contain virtual Hubbard double-occupancy excitations (as modeled in PRs #55–58) and additional electric/exchange compound faults; neither these Hubbard processes nor virtual gluon/quark-antiquark fluctuations are included in the present exact 14D control-algebra rank.
- **This is not a new result for full continuous SU(3), the nine-quark 21-dimensional code, or its 65-syndrome recovery.**
