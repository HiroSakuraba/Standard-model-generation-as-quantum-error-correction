# The full Standard Model centre echo

**Status, 19 October 2026:** exploratory note, not peer reviewed. Verified by `python3 sm_center_echo.py` (numpy and scipy; under a second; ends with `ALL SM CENTRE-ECHO CHECKS PASSED`).

**Question.** The cubic-Casimir echo in [`SU3_CUBIC_CASIMIR_REFOCUSING_AUDIT.md`](SU3_CUBIC_CASIMIR_REFOCUSING_AUDIT.md) uses the SU(3) centre: a pulse that multiplies every link-raising operator by ω = e^{2πi/3}, so three pulses average the magnetic Hamiltonian away. What happens when the same idea is applied to the whole gauge group SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1)_Y, with quarks and leptons hopping on the links?

**Answer.** Exactly one finite group of centre pulses leaves every Standard Model field untouched, and it is the ℤ6 that fixes the global form of the gauge group. Its eigenvalue is a syndrome for fractional electric charge. The right-handed neutrino is the one field that no centre pulse can reach.

## Setup

A link of a lattice gauge theory carries an irrep R of the gauge group. By Schur's lemma, the centre ℤ3 × ℤ2 × U(1) acts on R as a scalar. Label it by three numbers:

| Label | Meaning | Values |
|---|---|---|
| t | triality of the SU(3) irrep (p, q): t = p − q | mod 3 |
| d | N-ality of the SU(2) irrep: d = 2j | mod 2 |
| n | hypercharge flux, n = 6Y | integer |

A **centre pulse** is the diagonal link unitary

  P(a, b, θ) = ω^{at} (−1)^{bd} e^{iθn}.

A matter hop ψ†_x U_xy ψ_y in representation R_f takes the link from R to the components of R ⊗ R_f, all of which carry the label of R plus the label of f. So conjugating the hop by P multiplies it by P's character on f. That is exact; it does not depend on truncation. Averaging over a group of pulses keeps precisely the terms whose character is trivial on the group.

## Result 1: the ℤ6 relation

Every Standard Model field obeys

  6Y ≡ 3d − 2t (mod 6).

| Field | t | d | 6Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| u^c | 2 | 0 | −4 |
| d^c | 2 | 0 | 2 |
| L | 0 | 1 | −3 |
| e^c | 0 | 0 | 6 |
| ν^c | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| H | 0 | 1 | 3 |

## Result 2: the transparent group is exactly ℤ6

The centre elements that act trivially on every field form ℤ6, generated by (ω, −1, e^{2πi/6}). The quark doublet Q alone already fixes this group: its n = 1 forces e^{iθ} = ω^{−a}(−1)^{b}. After that, u^c, d^c, L, e^c, ν^c and the Higgs are transparent automatically. That is the content of the ℤ6 relation. With leptons and the Higgs alone, the transparent set is larger: 18 points on the script's 3 × 2 × 36 grid.

## Result 3: the echo table

Averaging over each pulse group keeps (K) or cancels (·) each term:

| Term | colour ℤ3 | weak ℤ2 | hypercharge U(1) | **SM ℤ6** | full centre |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SU(3) Wilson, fundamental | · | K | K | **·** | · |
| SU(2) Wilson, fundamental | K | · | K | **·** | · |
| U(1) Wilson, n = 1 | K | K | · | **·** | · |
| U(1) Wilson, n = 6 | K | K | · | **K** | · |
| hop Q | · | · | · | **K** | · |
| hop u^c, d^c | · | K | · | **K** | · |
| hop L, H | K | · | · | **K** | · |
| hop e^c | K | K | · | **K** | · |
| hop ν^c | K | K | K | **K** | K |
| Yukawa, electric energy | K | K | K | **K** | K |

The ℤ6 echo is the matter-blind one: it removes every fundamental Wilson term and keeps every Standard Model hop, every Yukawa coupling and the electric energy. The colour echo alone stops the quarks; the weak echo stops the doublets; the hypercharge echo stops everything charged.

## Result 4: ν^c is untouchable

ν^c has label (0, 0, 0). Every centre pulse is transparent to its hop, and so is every link-diagonal unitary, because the ν^c hop has no link to act on. It is the only field the full centre leaves moving. In the generation codes, ν^c is the punctured column that carries the logical qudit. In the echo it is the field no gauge-side control can address.

## Result 5: the echo eigenvalue is a fractional-charge syndrome

On every weight of every field, the ℤ6 generator acts as

  exp 2πi (Q_em + t/3),

and it equals 1 for all of them. Read as a stabilizer, ℤ6 says that colour singlets have integer electric charge and colour triplets have charge −t/3 mod 1. Measuring the echo eigenvalue on a link reports a fractionally charged colour singlet, or a bare SU(3), SU(2) or U(1) flux with no matter attached. Millikan's observation of integer charges is the statement that this syndrome has never fired.

## Result 6: lattices

On a lattice, a closed Wilson loop changes the oriented sum of link labels by zero, so a uniform pulse cannot cancel it. Weighting links fixes this. Give links along axis μ the weight x_{μ−1}, the coordinate along the preceding axis taken cyclically (in 2D the x-links get weight 0). Then every plaquette of a periodic 6^D lattice has oriented weight ±1 mod 6. The script checks all 36 plaquettes in 2D and all 648 in 3D.

With the pulse e^{2πik w_ℓ e₆(c_ℓ)/6}, where e₆ = 2t + 3d + n, every fundamental plaquette picks up a non-trivial phase. The phase is e^{±2πi·2/6} for SU(3), e^{±2πi·3/6} for SU(2) and e^{±2πi/6} for U(1). Every matter hop has e₆ = 0 on its link and stays transparent for any weights.

## Result 7: building the pulses from Casimirs

ω^t is a function of the SU(3) Casimirs. (C₂, C₃) separate all 45 irreps with p + q ≤ 8. The cubic-Casimir formula ω^{(9/10)C₃} from the qutrit chain agrees with ω^t on 1, 3, 3̄ and 8, but fails on the 6. A truncation that keeps the 6 needs the general function of (C₂, C₃). For SU(2), (−1)^{2j} is a function of C₂ = j(j+1); for U(1) the pulse is e^{iθE}.

## Result 8: numerical echo

The script builds one link register (ℤ3 × ℤ2 × {−7, …, 7}, dimension 90). It has a random electric energy, all five charged SM hops and the three fundamental Wilson terms. The 6-pulse ℤ6 average equals the electric energy plus matter hops exactly. A finite echo with total time T = 2 converges to the matter-only evolution:

| ℤ6 cycles | ‖U_echo − e^{−iH_matter T}‖ |
|---|---|
| 4 | 0.212 |
| 16 | 0.053 |
| 64 | 0.013 |

The error falls by a factor of 4.00 for each 4× increase in cycles, which is first-order average-Hamiltonian scaling.

## Scope

- **What is a model.** The fermion operators on sites are omitted from the numerical register because they commute with link pulses. The pulses are assumed controls, as in the SU(3) echo audit; nothing here is a hardware proposal.
- **What depends on the global form.** In the theory (SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1))/ℤ6, every allowed link irrep has trivial ℤ6 label, so this echo is the identity. It works as a control only if the quotient is smaller. [`GLOBAL_FORM_CODES.md`](GLOBAL_FORM_CODES.md) makes this precise.
